About

Brianna Myers

Hello! Welcome to my portfolio!

My name is Brianna, and I'm an artist currently studying Animation Production with a minor in Marketing at Ball State University. I enjoy working across both 2D and 3D animation, with a particular interest in character design and storyboarding. I'm drawn to experimentation, and exploring different ways to bring ideas to life through both digital and traditional mediums.

Ultimately, I hope to build a career in animation and create things that stick with people. I'm especially interested in bringing memorable characters and imaginative worlds to life, and hopefully making something that someone else grows up loving as much as I loved the things that inspired me.

Please enjoy your stay!
